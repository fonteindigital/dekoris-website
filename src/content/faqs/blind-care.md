title: "How do I clean and maintain my blinds?"
answer: "For most fabrics, light dusting with a microfibre cloth or vacuum brush attachment is enough. Honeycomb and Roman blinds occasionally benefit from a gentle wipe with a damp cloth. Venetian and aluminium slats can be cleaned slat-by-slat with a soft cloth. We walk you through care for your specific fabric at installation, and you can always call us if anything needs attention."
